# ARCAV — Project Context

## What ARCAV is

ARCAV is Armando Castro's professional brand and portfolio.

It should feel like a small **technical product studio** rather than a traditional CV website or freelancer landing page.

ARCAV represents work at the intersection of:

- Product
- Software engineering
- Process automation
- Systems integration
- Product thinking
- AI-assisted software delivery

## Core positioning

**Transformo procesos de negocio en productos digitales simples, automatizados y fáciles de operar.**

Supporting idea:

**Pequeños en alcance. Serios en ingeniería.**

Brand descriptor:

**ARCAV — Digital products & process evolution — by Armando Castro**

## Core concept

**Evolución tecnológica de procesos.**

Meaning:

Business processes often grow organically through spreadsheets, WhatsApp, forms, people, manual tasks, and disconnected systems.

ARCAV helps understand those processes, simplify them, connect what already works, and build the missing digital layer.

## Primary audience

ARCAV should work for:

- Small and medium businesses
- Marketing teams and freelancers
- Event-based businesses
- Restaurants
- Real estate
- Teams with operational processes that have outgrown manual tools
- Technical leaders and recruiters evaluating Armando's product/engineering profile

## Commercial focus

### 1. Connected landing pages
Landing pages connected to lead capture, notifications, CRM, databases, automation, and operational follow-up.

### 2. Small business systems
Backoffice, reservations, events, lightweight CRM, internal dashboards, orders, inventory, and operational tools.

### 3. Automation and integration
APIs, n8n, webhooks, databases, Sheets, forms, notifications, and existing systems.

## Product philosophy

- Start from the business problem, not the stack.
- Understand the current process before proposing replacement.
- Integrate before replacing when the existing tool still works.
- Avoid unnecessary coupling.
- Keep the operator in familiar territory when that improves adoption.
- Small scope does not mean weak engineering.
- Quality should be proportional to risk and context.
- Build, observe, measure, and improve.

## What ARCAV must not become

Avoid:

- "Hi, I'm a Full Stack Developer" as the main identity
- Technology-logo walls as the main proof of expertise
- Generic SaaS copy
- Marketing-agency language
- Fake studio/team positioning
- Invented metrics or testimonials
- Overly corporate tone
- Overly flashy sci-fi UI
